// Features
EVERYTHING YOU NEED

Built for students who want results, not complexity.

⚡
AI Flashcards
AI extracts the key terms and definitions from your content and turns them into a clean, flippable card deck.
🎯
Randomized Quiz
Every time you take the quiz, questions AND answer choices shuffle so you can't just memorize positions.
✏️
Fill in the Blank
Type-in-the-answer exercises force active recall — the most effective study technique backed by cognitive science.
🗃
Nested Workspaces
Create class-level workspaces, then nest modules inside (Chapter 1, Test 2, Week 3) — organize however you think.
✍️
Manual Editor
AI not perfect? Edit every card, question, and answer manually. Add your own, remove what's wrong, save instantly.
🔒
Your Account, Your Data
Sign up and your workspaces are saved to your account. Study from any device, pick up exactly where you left off.
